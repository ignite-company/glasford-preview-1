Helpful details for an estimate.

  • Your city or general neighborhood in Port St. Lucie or the West Palm Beach area.
  • Whether you are considering trimming, pruning or removing a tree.
  • The approximate number and size of the trees involved.
  • Any nearby fences, patios, power lines or structures that affect access.
  • Photos showing the full tree and surroundings, if available.

Questions before you reach out.

What should I share when asking for an estimate?

Let us know your city, the number of trees involved, what you are noticing and whether access to the tree is limited. Photos of the trees can also help explain the situation.

Does every tree need to be removed if it looks unhealthy?

Not necessarily. The right response depends on its condition, location and whether trimming or pruning may be appropriate. Discuss the tree before making a decision.

Do you work on residential properties?

Homeowners can request service information for their property. Please confirm the exact service and location when you reach out.
