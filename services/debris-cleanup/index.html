SOUTH FLORIDA TREE CARE

Tree branch cleanup and debris planning

Cleanup is part of a successful tree-care plan. Ask how cut limbs, logs and other material will be handled, whether hauling is included, and what condition the site should be left in.

Ask about an estimate ↗

What to consider.

01

Clarify the estimate

Confirm which kinds of cleanup and material removal are included.

02

Consider larger projects

Ask specifically about logs, mulch, chips, stump work and any separate services.

03

Plan access

Gates, fences and tight outdoor spaces can affect handling of branches.

Frequently asked questions.

Is debris removal always included?

Do not assume it is. Ask for the cleanup scope to appear in the estimate.

Can storm debris be handled?

Describe the debris and ask whether the project fits the team's current scope and schedule.
