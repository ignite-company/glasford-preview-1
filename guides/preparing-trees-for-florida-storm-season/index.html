SOUTH FLORIDA TREE CARE

Preparing your trees for Florida storm season

A homeowner guide to recognizing concerns and planning tree care before storm weather.

Ask about an estimate ↗

Look at trees ahead of time

Identify visible damaged or rubbing branches and any significant changes to the tree.

Avoid topping and panic cutting

Severe cuts can harm a tree. The right work depends on its species, structure and condition.

Stay away from utility hazards

Never attempt to cut branches near energized lines. Contact your local utility or authorized professionals.

Discuss expectations

Confirm project scope, availability, access and whether debris handling is included.
