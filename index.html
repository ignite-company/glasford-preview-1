MORE WAYS TO HELP

More ways to care for your property.

Explore palm care, branches, and practical tree-care needs. Scope and availability are confirmed with Glasford.

Palm tree care

Palm trees require a different approach from broadleaf trees. Ask about dead fronds, overcrowded canopies and maintaining attractive palms without unnecessary cutting.

Learn more ↗

Storm preparation

Plan conversations about broken branches, mature canopies and preventative tree work before severe weather. No tree can be made storm-proof, and the right approach depends on its condition.

Learn more ↗

Branch clearance

Overgrown limbs can interfere with walkways, gutters, driveways and outdoor spaces. Share what the branches are affecting and ask which selective work may be appropriate.

Learn more ↗

Branch & debris cleanup

Cleanup is part of a successful tree-care plan. Ask how cut limbs, logs and other material will be handled, whether hauling is included, and what condition the site should be left in.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few things to know.

Answers to common questions before requesting an estimate.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, you can request a free project estimate and discuss the type of tree work you need.

Can you trim branches that are growing close to my house?

Describe the branches and their location when requesting an estimate. The work and approach can be discussed after assessing the job.

Do you work on more than one tree at a time?

You can ask about multiple trees within one project. Share the number of trees and what each one needs.

What is the difference between trimming and pruning?

Tree trimming often addresses overgrowth or clearance, while pruning focuses on selective branch removal and tree structure. The right work depends on the tree and your goals.

Do you work on residential and commercial properties?

Both residential and commercial inquiries are welcome. Confirm project fit, access, and service availability with Glasford.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Port St. Lucie, West Palm Beach, and surrounding areas of Palm Beach County. Request an estimate to confirm whether your property is within our current service area.
